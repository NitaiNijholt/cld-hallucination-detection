================================================================================
KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

1. The LLM generator significantly outperforms random baseline across all CLDs
   (2.4× to 3.7× improvement), confirming meaningful causal structure recovery.

2. Social Norms shows the largest relative improvement (3.7×) despite having
   the smallest validation CLD, suggesting the generator performs well on
   compact causal structures.

3. Emergency Department shows the lowest absolute F1 (0.131) and smallest
   improvement (2.4×), reflecting the challenge of recovering edges in
   larger, sparser graphs (66 edges across 36 nodes, density 5.2%).

4. The 95% confidence intervals demonstrate consistent performance across
   generation runs, with Depressive Symptoms showing the tightest bounds
   (0.48-0.58), indicating reliable edge recovery.
