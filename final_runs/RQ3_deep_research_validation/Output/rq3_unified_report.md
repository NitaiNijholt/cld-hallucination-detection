## Methodology

### Deep Research System Design

The Deep Research system employs principles from LLM multi-agent literature:
- **Iterative refinement:** Self-refine search agent (Madaan et al., 2023)
- **Independent search directions:** Chain-of-Verification (Dhuliawala et al., 2024)
- **Parallel execution:** Test-time compute scaling (Snell et al., 2024)
- **Mandatory passage citing:** SAFE system (Batista et al., 2025)

### Statistical Methods

- **Fisher's Exact Test:** Primary test for 2×2 contingency tables
- **Chi-Square Test:** With assumption checking (Cochran's rule)
- **Cohen's h:** Effect size for proportion differences with 95% CI
- **Wilson Score Interval:** 95% CI for proportions
- **Bonferroni Correction:** For multiple pairwise comparisons (m=3)
- **Post-hoc Power Analysis:** To assess adequacy of sample size

## Limitations

1. **TP-FP Discrimination:** The gap (8.1 pp) is not statistically significant; DR cannot reliably distinguish valid from hallucinated edges
2. **Domain Variation:** FP detection rates vary substantially by CLD (28%–76%)
3. **Correlational Confound:** DR may detect correlational rather than causal evidence
4. **No Component Ablation:** Individual component contributions not isolated
5. **Single Run:** No DR replication for uncertainty quantification
6. **Strong Assumptions:** Evidence evaluated in isolation, not in full CLD context
